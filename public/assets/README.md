# Game assets

The characters and baskets supplied in `HalloweenFiles.zip` are copied into their matching folders and loaded by the current filenames. They are 32 x 32 transparent PNGs. See the folder READMEs for the exact image lists.

Pet art from `Sprites.zip` is in `pets/` with simplified names: `hedwig.png`, `snitch.png`, `cat.png`, `bat.png`, and `dog.png`. Dog and cat sprites appear on the ground beside the player. Pigeon remains a code-drawn placeholder until its art is added. House art is still drawn in code until house PNGs are added as `houses/house-1.png` through `house-15.png`.

- `music/menu.mp3` is the existing menu music and remains unchanged.
- `music/street.mp3` now contains the supplied `WMYBSong.mp3` and plays on the street.

The moon, stars, rangoli, marigold lights, skyline, pavement, lanterns, shop icons, interface, and effects are drawn in Phaser code.
